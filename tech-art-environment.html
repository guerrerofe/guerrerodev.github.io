Comprehensive Tech Art Integration

This project is a culmination of multiple Technical Art disciplines combined into a single interactive environment in Unreal Engine 5. The primary focus was to build dynamic materials from scratch, establish efficient rendering parameters, and populate the scene with custom visual effects and modular geometry.

Systems Implemented

  • Dynamic Water Simulation: Engineered a custom ocean shader using panning normal maps with modified UV coordinates to simulate realistic, moving water surfaces.
  • Material Authoring: Developed complex master materials and instances, utilizing grayscale masks (created in Photoshop) to control roughness, metallic properties, and color tinting across different surfaces.
  • Modular Mesh Creation: Modeled and manipulated basic geometry directly within the engine to construct cohesive, modular architectural pieces (columns, walls, roofs).
  • Niagara VFX: Integrated particle systems using sprite sheet animations to generate dynamic environmental effects such as lightning and sparks.
