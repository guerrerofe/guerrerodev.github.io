Project Information

  • Role: UI Programmer & Build Manager
  • Engine: Unity
  • Genre: 2.5D Platformer
  • Context: Game Jam Project

Thriving Under Pressure

Beyond The Dust is a 2.5D platformer born out of my first-ever Game Jam experience. Working in a small, tight-knit team, the project was a crash course in rapid prototyping, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and strict time management under extreme deadlines.

Tasks Performed

  • UI & Menus: Developed and integrated the Heads-Up Display and core navigational menus within a highly compressed timeframe.
  • Audio Production: Took the initiative to source, record, and edit the game's music and sound effects (SFX)—a completely new skill set for me—ensuring all assets were production-ready.
  • Cross-Discipline Support: Stepped outside my primary role to troubleshoot systems and assist teammates with engine implementation to compensate for our limited team size.
  • Release Management: Took responsibility for compiling, preparing, and verifying the final submission build just before the deadline.

The Challenge & Learning

Game Jams are a test of scope management. Our initial vision was highly ambitious, and we soon realized we lacked the personnel to finish the entire concept. In the final hours, I had to make the difficult call to prioritize compiling a stable, playable build over implementing the audio assets I had spent time producing. While the game shipped without its final soundscapes, the experience was incredibly rewarding. It massively improved my ability to work under high pressure, reinforced both my individual and team organization skills, and allowed me to connect with amazing talent in the community.
