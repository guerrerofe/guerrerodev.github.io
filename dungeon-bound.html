Project Information

  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Engine: Unreal Engine 5
  • Genre: Co-op Puzzle Adventure
  • Tools: Perforce, Blueprints/C++

Restoring the Light

Dungeon Bound: The Last Chamber is a cooperative puzzle game where two protagonists must combine their unique abilities to navigate a treacherous dungeon. The ultimate goal is to reach the final chamber, defeat the main boss, and successfully restore light to their world.

Tasks Performed

  • Gameplay & Puzzle Logic: Programmed core gameplay mechanics and interactive puzzle systems required for cooperative progression.
  • Level Design: Designed and assembled the dungeon layout to ensure balanced pacing and engaging co-op challenges.
  • AI & Enemy Functionality: Implemented enemy behavior trees, combat interactions, and the final boss mechanics.
  • Character Mechanics: Developed the complete functionality, movement, and specific ability set for the character Jowyrel.

The Challenge & Learning

Developed alongside a partner, this project served as my introduction to cooperative multiplayer development in Unreal Engine 5. It significantly expanded my technical knowledge of the engine's framework and gave me crucial hands-on experience managing project versions and collaboration using industry-standard tools like Perforce.
