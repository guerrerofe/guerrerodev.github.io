About Me

From gamer to developer

Hey! I'm Juan Guerrero, and I've been an absolute video game geek since I was a kid. Growing up enjoying them so much naturally led me to study Video Game Engineering at ESAT here in my hometown, Valencia.

During my time at school, my curiosity about the inner workings of games kept growing until I discovered the exact path I wanted to specialize in: Technical Art. I fell in love with that sweet spot where hardcore programming meets the creative pipeline. I find it absolutely fascinating how this role acts as the perfect bridge between artists and programmers, and I'm super excited to keep specializing and growing as a Tech Artist.

When my curiosity isn't driving me to learn new things about game development, you'll probably find me playing almost any kind of sport, reading a good book, or just enjoying nature. Got a cool project in mind or just want to chat about games? Let's connect!
