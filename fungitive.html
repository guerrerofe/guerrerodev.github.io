Project Information

  • Role: UI/UX Programmer & Audio Integrator
  • Engine: Unity
  • Genre: Infinite Runner
  • Tools: C#, Unity UI System

The Great Escape

Fungitive is a fast-paced infinite runner where players take control of a restless mushroom desperately fleeing from a farmer. Born from complete creative freedom during its conceptualization, the game focuses on quick reflexes and engaging audio-visual feedback.

Tasks Performed

  • UI & Menu Architecture: Programmed the complete navigation flow, including the main menu, pause screens, and dynamic game-over states.
  • HUD Implementation: Developed the real-time Heads-Up Display to track scores and essential player feedback without cluttering the screen.
  • Audio Integration: Handled the logic and triggering for background music and sound effects (SFX) to enhance the pacing and immersion of the runner mechanics.

The Challenge & Learning

Co-developed with a classmate, this project was a turning point for my technical skill set. While handling the front-end elements, I discovered a strong passion for UI/UX programming. Designing systems that communicate clearly with the player while maintaining a clean codebase taught me the importance of interface architecture in game development.
