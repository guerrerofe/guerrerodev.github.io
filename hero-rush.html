Project Information

  • Role: Technical Artist & UI Programmer
  • Engine: Unity
  • Genre: Top-Down Roguelike
  • Tools: C#, Unity Animation/UI, Rigging

Surviving the Iterative Journey

Hero Rush: Odyssey is an action-packed top-down roguelike developed continuously throughout my second academic year. Designed with an emphasis on replayability, the project required robust systems to handle dynamic gameplay loops, fluid animations, and clear player feedback.

Tasks Performed

  • Technical Art & Animation: Edited textures, managed character skeletons (rigging), and integrated complex animation state machines.
  • UI & HUD Architecture: Designed and programmed the in-game menus and real-time Heads-Up Display to ensure seamless user interaction.
  • Level Design: Conceptualized and built the environments to support top-down combat mechanics and roguelike progression.
  • Audio Integration: Implemented the music and sound effects (SFX) to provide satisfying combat feedback and atmosphere.

The Challenge & Learning

Co-developed with a classmate, this project holds a special place in my development journey due to our outstanding organization and collaborative environment. It taught me the immense value of effective teamwork and ultimately sparked my ambition to specialize as a Technical Artist. Troubleshooting and resolving critical visual pipeline conflicts—specifically balancing character rigging, texture optimization, and animation implementation—provided me with invaluable hands-on experience in bridging the gap between art and programming.
